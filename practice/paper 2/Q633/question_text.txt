12. [Maximum mark: 19]

Consider the differential equation x² dy/dx = y³ - 2x³ for x > 0 and y > 2x. It is given that y = 3 when x = 1.

(a) Use Euler's method, with a step length of 0.1, to find an approximate value of y when x = 1.5. [4]

(b) Use the substitution y = vx to show that x dy/dx = v³ - v - 2. [3]

(c) (i) By solving the differential equation, show that y = 8x + x⁴ / 4 - x³.
(ii) Find the actual value of y when x = 1.5.
(iii) Using the graph of y = 8x + x⁴ / 4 - x³, suggest a reason why the approximation given by Euler's method in part (a) is not a good estimate to the actual value of y at x = 1.5. [12]
