5. [Maximum mark: 6]

The arithmetic sequence {un : n ∈ ℤ+} has first term u1 = 1.6 and common 
difference d = 1.5. The geometric sequence {vn : n ∈ ℤ+} has first term v1 = 3 and
common ratio r = 1.2.

(a) Find an expression for un - vn in terms of n. [2 marks]

(b) Determine the set of values of n for which un > vn. [3 marks]

(c) Determine the greatest value of un - vn. Give your answer correct to four
significant figures. [1 mark]
