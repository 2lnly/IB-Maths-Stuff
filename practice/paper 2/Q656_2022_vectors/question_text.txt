11. [Maximum mark: 15] 
The function f is defined by f(x) = e^(3(x - 4)), where x ∈ R.
(a) Find f'(x).
(b) Hence or otherwise, find the coordinates of the point on the graph of y = f(x) where the tangent is parallel to the line y = x.
The region enclosed by the curve y = f(x), the x-axis and the y-axis is rotated through 2π radians about the x-axis to form a solid of revolution.
(c) Find the volume of this solid.
Consider a function g such that g(0) = 1 and g'(0) = 2.
(d) Find the value of
(i) (f ° g)(0);
(ii) (f ° g)'(0).

12. [Maximum mark: 22]
Consider the points A(1, 2, 3), B(k, -2, 1) and C(5, 0, 2), where k ∈ R.
(a) Write down AB and AC.
(b) Given that the points A, B and C lie on a straight line, show that k = 9.
(c) For k = 9, let L1 be the line passing through A, B and C.
(i) Find a vector equation of the line L1.
(ii) Line L2 has the equation (x-1)/2 = y/3 = 1-z. Show that the lines L1 and L2 are skew.
(d) For k ≠ 9, let Π be the plane containing A, B and C.
(i) Find the Cartesian equation of the plane Π.
(ii) Find the coordinates of the point on the plane Π which is closest to the origin (0, 0, 0).
