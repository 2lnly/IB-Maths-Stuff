9. [Maximum mark: 5]

Let S be the set of 30 positive integers {1, 2, 3, ..., 28, 29, 30}.

Raghu randomly selects three positive integers from S without replacement. He then adds them together and determines whether the sum is divisible by 3.

Determine the total number of selections Raghu can make to obtain a sum that is divisible by 3.

You may assume that order is not important, for example, {1, 2, 3}, {1, 3, 2}, {2, 3, 1}, {2, 1, 3}, {3, 1, 2}, {3, 2, 1} are all considered to be the same selection. [5]
