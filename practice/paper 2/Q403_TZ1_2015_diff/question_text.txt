A bicycle inner tube can be considered as a joined up cylinder of fixed length 200 cm
and radius r cm. The radius r increases as the inner tube is pumped up. Air is being
pumped into the inner tube so that the volume of air in the tube increases at a constant rate
of 30 cm^3 s^-1. Find the rate at which the radius of the inner tube is increasing when r = 2 cm.
