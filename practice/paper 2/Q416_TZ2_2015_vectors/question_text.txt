Consider the vectors given by u = i + 2j - 2k and v = ai + bj, where a and b are constants.

It is given that u × v = 4i + bj + ck, where c is a constant.

(a) Find the value of each of the constants a, b and c. [5]
(b) Hence find the Cartesian equation of the plane containing the vectors u and v and passing through the point (0, 0, 0). [2]
