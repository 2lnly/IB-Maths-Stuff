A factory manufactures lamps. It is known that the probability that a lamp is found to be defective is 0.05. A random sample of 30 lamps is tested.

(a) Find the probability that there is at least one defective lamp in the sample.

(b) Given that there is at least one defective lamp in the sample, find the probability that there are at most two defective lamps.
