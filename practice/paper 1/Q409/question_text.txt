9. [Maximum mark: 17]

Let f(x) = (2 - 3x^3)/2x^3, x ∈ ℝ, x ≠ 0.

(a) The graph of y = f(x) has a local maximum at A. Find the coordinates of A.  [5]

(b) (i) Show that there is exactly one point of inflexion, B, on the graph of y = f(x).
    (ii) The coordinates of B can be expressed in the form B(2^a, b × 2^-3a), 
         where a, b ∈ ℚ. Find the value of a and the value of b.  [8]

(c) Sketch the graph of y = f(x) showing clearly the position of the points A and B.  [4]
