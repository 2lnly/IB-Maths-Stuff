10. [Maximum mark: 20]

The function f is given by f(x) = x^(-x) (x ≥ 0).

(a) (i) Find an expression for f'(x).
    (ii) Hence determine the coordinates of the point A, where f'(x) = 0.

(b) Find an expression for f''(x) and hence show the point A is a maximum.

(c) Find the coordinates of B, the point of inflexion.

(d) The graph of the function g is obtained from the graph of f by stretching it in the x-direction by a scale factor 2.
    (i) Write down an expression for g(x).
    (ii) State the coordinates of the maximum C of g.
    (iii) Determine the x-coordinates of D and E, the two points where f(x) = g(x).

(e) Sketch the graphs of y = f(x) and y = g(x) on the same axes, showing clearly the points A, B, C, D and E.

(f) Find an exact value for the area of the region bounded by the curve y = g(x), the x-axis and the line x = 1.
