11. [Maximum mark: 20]

Consider the points A(1, 0, 0), B(2, 2, 2) and C(0, 2, 1).

(a)   Find the vector CA × CB.                                               [4]

(b)   Find an exact value for the area of the triangle ABC.        [3] 

(c)   Show that the Cartesian equation of the plane Π1, containing the triangle ABC,
is 2x + 3y - 4z = 2.                                                           [3]

A second plane Π2 is defined by the Cartesian equation Π2: 4x - y - z = 4. L1 is the line of
intersection of the planes Π1 and Π2.

(d)   Find a vector equation for L1.                                          [5]

A third plane Π3 is defined by the Cartesian equation 16x + αy - 3z = β.

(e)   Find the value of α if all three planes contain L1.                [3]

(f)   Find conditions on α and β if the plane Π3 does not intersect with L1.        [2]
