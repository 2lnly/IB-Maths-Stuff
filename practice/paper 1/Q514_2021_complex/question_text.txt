Consider the equation (z - 1)3 = i, z ∈ C. The roots of this equation are ω1, ω2 and ω3, where Im(ω2) > 0 and Im(ω3) < 0.

(a) (i) Verify that ω1 = 1+e^(2πi/3) is a root of this equation.
(ii) Find ω2 and ω3, expressing these in the form a + ei∞, where a ∈ ℝ and ∞ > 0. [6]

The roots ω1, ω2 and ω3 are represented by the points A, B and C respectively on an Argand diagram.

(b) Plot the points A, B and C on an Argand diagram. [4]
(c) Find AC. [3]

Consider the equation (z - 1)3 = i3, z ∈ C.

(d) By using de Moivre's theorem, show that α = 1/(1-e^(2πi/3)) is a root of this equation. [3]
(e) Determine the value of Re(α). [6]
