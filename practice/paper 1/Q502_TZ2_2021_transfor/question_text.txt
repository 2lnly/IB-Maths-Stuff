12. [Maximum mark: 19]

The following diagram shows the graph of y = arctan(2x+1) + π/4 for x ∈ ℝ, with asymptotes at y = -π/4 and y = 3π/4.

(a) Describe a sequence of transformations that transforms the graph of y = arctan x to the graph of y = arctan(2x+1) + π/4 for x ∈ ℝ. [3]

(b) Show that arctan p + arctan q ≡ arctan ((p+q)/(1-pq)) where p, q > 0 and pq < 1. [4]

(c) Verify that arctan(2x+1) = arctan ((x+1)/π + 1/4) for x ∈ ℝ, x > 0. [3]

(d) Using mathematical induction and the result from part (b), prove that

Σ arctan (1/2r²) = arctan (n/(n+1)) for n ∈ ℤ⁺. [9]
