12. [Maximum mark: 18]

Let zn be the complex number defined as zn = (n2 + n + 1) + i for n ∈ N.

(a) (i) Find arg(z0).
    (ii) Write down an expression for arg(zn) in terms of n.                                  [3]

Let wn = z0z1z2...zn-1 for n ∈ N.

(b) (i) Show that arctan(a) + arctan(b) = arctan ((a + b) / (1 - ab)) for a, b ∈ ℝ, ab < 1.
    (ii) Hence or otherwise, show that arg(wn) = arctan(2).                                  [5]

(c) Prove by mathematical induction that arg(wn) = arctan(n + 1) for n ∈ N.        [10]
