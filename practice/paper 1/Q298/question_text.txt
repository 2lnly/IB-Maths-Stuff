(a) Show that g ◦ f(x) = 3sin(2x + π/5) + 4.

(b) Find the range of g ◦ f.

(c) Given that g ◦ f(3π/20) = 7, find the next value of x, greater than 3π/20, for which g ◦ f(x) = 7.

(d) The graph of y = g ◦ f(x) can be obtained by applying four transformations to the graph of y = sin x. State what the four transformations represent geometrically and give the order in which they are applied.
