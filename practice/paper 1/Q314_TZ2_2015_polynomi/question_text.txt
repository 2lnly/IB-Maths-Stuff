12. [Maximum mark: 14]
The cubic equation x^3 + px^2 + qx + c = 0 , has roots α, β, γ. By expanding (x - α)(x - β)(x - γ) show that
(a)   (i) p = -(α + β + γ);
     (ii) q = αβ + βγ + γα;
     (iii) c = -αβγ.
It is now given that p = -6 and q = 18 for parts (b) and (c) below.
(b)   (i) In the case that the three roots α, β, γ form an arithmetic sequence, show that one of the roots is 2.
     (ii) Hence determine the value of c. [5]
(c)   In another case the three roots α, β, γ form a geometric sequence. Determine the value of c. [6]
