9. [Maximum mark: 15]
Consider a triangle OAB such that O has coordinates (0, 0, 0), A has coordinates (0, 1, 2) and B has coordinates (2b, 0, b - 1) where b < 0.

(a) Find, in terms of b, a Cartesian equation of the plane Π containing this triangle. [5]

Let M be the midpoint of the line segment [OB].

(b) Find, in terms of b, the equation of the line L which passes through M and is perpendicular to the plane Π. [3]

(c) Show that L does not intersect the y-axis for any negative value of b. [7]
