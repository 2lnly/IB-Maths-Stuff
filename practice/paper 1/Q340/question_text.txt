12. [Maximum mark: 21]

(a) Use de Moivre's theorem to find the value of cos(π/3) + i sin(π/3)^3.

(b) Use mathematical induction to prove that
(cos θ - i sin θ)^n = cos nθ - i sin nθ for n ∈ Z.
Let z = cos θ + i sin θ.

(c) Find an expression in terms of θ for (z)^n + (z^*)^n, n ∈ Z* where z* is the complex conjugate of z.

(d) (i) Show that zz* = 1.
(ii) Write down the binomial expansion of (z + z*)^3 in terms of z and z*.
(iii) Hence show that cos 3θ = 4 cos^3 θ - 3 cos θ.

(e) Hence solve 4 cos^3 θ - 2 cos^2 θ - 3 cos θ + 1 = 0 for 0 ≤ θ < π.
