2.  [Maximum mark: 30]

In this question, you will investigate the maximum product of positive real numbers with a given sum.

Consider the two numbers x1, x2 ∈ R+, such that x1 + x2 = 12.

(a)   Find the product of x1 and x2 as a function, f, of x1 only.  [2]

(b)   (i)   Find the value of x1 for which the function is maximum.  [1]
     (ii)   Hence show that the maximum product of x1 and x2 is 36.  [1]

Consider M_n(S) to be the maximum product of n positive real numbers with a sum of S, where n ∈ Z⁺ and S ∈ R⁺.

For n = 2, the maximum product can be expressed as M_2(S) = (S/2)².

(c)   Verify that M_2(S) = (S/2)² is true for S = 12.  [1]

Consider n positive real numbers, x1, x2, ..., x_n.

The geometric mean is defined as (x1 x x2 x ... x x_n)^(1/n). It is given that the geometric mean is always less than or equal to the arithmetic mean, so (x1 x x2 x ... x x_n)^(1/n) ≤ ((x1 + x2 + ... + x_n)/n).

(d)   (i)   Show that the geometric mean and arithmetic mean are equal when x1 = x2 = ... = x_n.  [2]
     (ii)   Use this result to prove that M_n(S) = (S/n)^n.  [4]

(e)   Hence determine the value of
     (i)   M_3(12);  [1]
     (ii)   M_4(12);  [1]
     (iii)  M_5(12).  [1]

For n ∈ Z⁺, let P(S) denote the maximum value of M_n(S) across all possible values of n.

(f)   Write down the value of P(12) and the value of n at which it occurs.  [2]

(g)   Determine the value of P(20) and the value of n at which it occurs.  [3]
