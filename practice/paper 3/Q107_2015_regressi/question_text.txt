1. [Maximum mark: 7]

It is known that the standard deviation of the heights of men in a certain country is 15.0 cm.

(a) One hundred men from that country, selected at random, had their heights measured. 
The mean of this sample was 185 cm. Calculate a 95% confidence interval for the 
mean height of the population.  [3]

(b) A second random sample of size n is taken from the same population. Find the 
minimum value of n needed for the width of a 95% confidence interval to be less 
than 3 cm.  [4]
