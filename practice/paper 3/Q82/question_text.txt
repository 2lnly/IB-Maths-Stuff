The random variable X is normally distributed with unknown mean μ and unknown
variance σ². A random sample of 20 observations on X gave the following results.

∑x = 280, ∑x² = 3977.57

(a)	Find unbiased estimates of μ and σ².					[3 marks]

(b)	Determine a 95 % confidence interval for μ.				[3 marks]

(c)	Given the hypotheses
	H0: μ = 15; H1: μ ≠ 15, 
find the p-value of the above results and state your conclusion at the 1 %
significance level.								[4 marks]
