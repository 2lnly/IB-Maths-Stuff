The random variables  X1  and  X2  are a random sample from  N(µ , 2σ 2) .  The random 
variables  Y1 , Y2  and  Y3  are a random sample from  N(2µ , σ 2) .
	
The estimator  U  is used to estimate  µ  where  U = a(X1 + X2) + b(Y1 + Y2 + Y3)  and  a , b  are 
constants.
	
(a)	
Given that  U  is unbiased, show that  2a + 6b = 1 .
[3]
	
(b)	
Show that  Var(U ) = (39b2 - 12b + 1)σ 2 .
[3]
	
(c)	
Hence find
	
	
(i)	
the value of  a  and the value of  b  which give the best unbiased estimator of this 
form, giving your answers as fractions.
	
	
(ii)	
the variance of this best unbiased estimator.
