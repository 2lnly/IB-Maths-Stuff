The weight of tea in Supermug tea bags has a normal distribution with mean 4.2 g 
and standard deviation 0.15 g.  The weight of tea in Megamug tea bags has a normal 
distribution with mean 5.6 g and standard deviation 0.17 g.
	
(a)	
Find the probability that a randomly chosen Supermug tea bag contains more 
than 3.9 g of tea.
[2 marks]
	
(b)	
Find the probability that, of two randomly chosen Megamug tea bags, 
one contains more than 5.4 g of tea and one contains less than 5.4 g of tea.
[4 marks]
	
(c)	
Find the probability that five randomly chosen Supermug tea bags contain a total 
of less than 20.5 g of tea.
[4 marks]
	
(d)	
Find the probability that the total weight of tea in seven randomly chosen 
Supermug tea bags is more than the total weight in five randomly chosen 
Megamug tea bags.
[5 marks]
