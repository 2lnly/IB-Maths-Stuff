Two independent discrete random variables  X  and  Y  have probability generating 
functions  G(t)  and  H(t)  respectively.  Let  Z = X + Y  have probability generating 
function  J(t) .
	
(a)	
Write down an expression for  J(t)  in terms of  G(t)  and  H(t) .
[1]
	
(b)	
By differentiating  J(t) , prove that
	
	
(i)	
E(Z ) = E(X ) + E(Y ) ;
	
	
(ii)	
Var(Z ) = Var(X ) + Var(Y ) .
