Let  G  be a finite cyclic group.
	
(a)	
Prove that  G  is Abelian.
[4 marks]
	
(b)	
Given that  a  is a generator of  G , show that a−1 is also a generator.
[5 marks]
	
(c)	
Show that if the order of  G  is five, then all elements of  G , apart from the identity, 
are generators of  G .
[4 marks]
