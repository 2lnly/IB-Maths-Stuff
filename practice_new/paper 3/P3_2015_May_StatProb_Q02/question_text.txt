Eleven students who had under-performed in a philosophy practice examination were given 
extra tuition before their final examination.  The differences between their final examination 
marks and their practice examination marks were
10 , −1 , 6 , 7 , −5 , −5 , 2 , −3 , 8 , 9 , −2 .
Assume that these differences form a random sample from a normal distribution with mean  µ  
and variance  σ 2 .
	
(a)	
Determine unbiased estimates of  µ  and  σ 2 .
[4]
	
(b)	
(i)	
State suitable hypotheses to test the claim that extra tuition improves examination 
marks.
	
	
(ii)	
Calculate the  p-value of the sample.
	
	
(iii)	
Determine whether or not the above claim is supported at the 5 % significance 
level.
