An Abelian group,  {G , *} , has  12  different elements which are of the form  ai * b  j  where  
i ∈ {1 , 2 , 3 , 4}  and  j ∈ {1 , 2 , 3} .  The elements  a  and  b  satisfy  a4 = e  and  b3 = e  where  
e  is the identity.
	
(a)	
State the possible orders of an element of  {G , *}  and for each order give an example 
of an element of that order.
[8]
	
Let  {H , *}  be the proper subgroup of  {G , *}  having the maximum possible order.
	
(b)	
(i)	
State a generator for  {H , *} .
	
	
(ii)	
Write down the elements of  {H , *} .
	
	
(iii)	
Write down the elements of the coset of  H  containing  a .
[7]
