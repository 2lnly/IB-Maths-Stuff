Eric plays a game at a fairground in which he throws darts at a target.  Each time he throws 
a dart, the probability of hitting the target is 0.2.  He is allowed to throw as many darts as 
he likes, but it costs him $1 a throw.  If he hits the target a total of three times he wins $10. 
	
(a)	
Find the probability he has his third success of hitting the target on his sixth throw.
[3]
	
(b)	
(i)	
Find the expected number of throws required for Eric to hit the target three times.
 
 
(ii) 
Write down his expected profit or loss if he plays until he wins the $10.
[3]
 
(c) 
If he has just $8, find the probability he will lose all his money before he hits the target 
three times.
[3]
