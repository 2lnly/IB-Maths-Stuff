Two students are selected at random from a large school with equal numbers of boys  
and girls.  The boys’ heights are normally distributed with mean 178 cm and standard 
deviation 5.2 cm, and the girls’ heights are normally distributed with mean 169 cm and 
standard deviation 5.4 cm.
Calculate the probability that the taller of the two students selected is a boy.
