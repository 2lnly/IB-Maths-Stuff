The daily rainfall in a holiday resort follows a normal distribution with mean µ mm 
and standard deviation σ mm.  The rainfall each day is independent of the rainfall on 
other days.
	
	
On a randomly chosen day, there is a probability of  0.05  that the rainfall is greater 
than 10.2 mm.
	
	
In a randomly chosen 7-day week, there is a probability of  0.025  that the mean daily 
rainfall is less than 6.1 mm.
	
Find the value of µ  and of σ .
