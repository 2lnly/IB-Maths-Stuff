Let the Maclaurin series for  tan x  be
tan x = a1 x + a3 x3 + a5 x5 + …
where  a1 , a3  and  a5  are constants.
	
(a)	
Find series for  sec2 x , in terms of  a1 , a3  and  a5 , up to and including the  x4  term
	
	
(i)	
by differentiating the above series for  tan x ;
	
	
(ii)	
by using the relationship  sec2 x = 1 + tan2 x .

(b)	
Hence, by comparing your two series, determine the values of  a1 , a3  and  a5 .
