A drinks machine sells cans of Ecoglug and Glucofizz.  The number of cans 
of Ecoglug and Glucofizz sold during a fixed period of time have Poisson 
distributions with means 8 per hour and 12 per hour, respectively.
	
(a)	
Find the probability that in a given 15 minute period fewer than 3 cans of 
Ecoglug are sold.
[2 marks]
	
(b)	
Find the probability that in a given 30 minute period more than 12 cans of drinks 
are sold.
[3 marks]
	
(c)	
In a given 5 minute period, exactly 2 cans are sold.  Find the probability that 
they are both Glucofizz.
[7 marks]
