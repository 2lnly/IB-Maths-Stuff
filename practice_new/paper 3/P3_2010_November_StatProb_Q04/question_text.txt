A teacher has forgotten his computer password.  He knows that it is either six of the 
letter J followed by two of the letter R (i.e. JJJJJJRR) or three of the letter J followed 
by four of the letter R (i.e. JJJRRRR).  The computer is able to tell him at random just 
two of the letters in his password.
The teacher decides to use the following rule to attempt to find his password.
If the computer gives him a J and a J, he will accept the null hypothesis that his 
password is JJJJJJRR.
Otherwise he will accept the alternative hypothesis that his password is JJJRRRR.
	
(a)	
Define a Type I error.
[1 mark]
	
(b)	
Find the probability that the teacher makes a Type I error.
[4 marks]
	
(c)	
Define a Type II error.
[1 mark]
	
(d)	
Find the probability that the teacher makes a Type II error.
[3 marks]
