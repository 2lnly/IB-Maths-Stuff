16.	
The lengths of a particular species of lizard are normally distributed with a mean length of 
50 cm  and a standard deviation of  4 cm .  A lizard is chosen at random.
	
(a)	
Find the probability that its length is greater than  45 cm .
(b)	
Given that its length is greater than  45 cm , find the probability that its length is greater 
than  55 cm .
