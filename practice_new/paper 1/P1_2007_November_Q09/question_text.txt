width is greater than 10.5 cm and undersize if its width is less than 9.5 cm.  From past 
experience it is found that 2 % of the table legs that are made are oversize and that 4  % 
of the table legs are undersize.  The widths of the table legs are normally distributed 
with mean µ  cm and standard deviation σ  cm.  Find the value of µ  and of σ .
