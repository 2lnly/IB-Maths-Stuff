Bag 1 contains 4 red cubes and 5 blue cubes.  Bag 2 contains 7 red cubes and 2 blue cubes.	
	
Two cubes are drawn at random, the first from Bag 1 and the second from Bag 2.
	
(a)	
Find the probability that the cubes are of the same colour.
	
(b)	
Given that the cubes selected are of different colours, find the probability that the red 
cube was selected from Bag 1.
