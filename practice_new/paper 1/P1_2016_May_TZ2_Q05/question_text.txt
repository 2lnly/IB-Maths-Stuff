[Maximum mark:  8]
	
A biased coin is tossed five times.  The probability of obtaining a head in any one throw is  p .
	
Let  X  be the number of heads obtained.
	
(a)	
Find, in terms of  p , an expression for  P(X = 4) .
[2]
	
(b)	
(i)	
Determine the value of  p  for which  P(X = 4)  is a maximum.
	
	
(ii)	
For this value of  p , determine the expected number of heads.
[6]
