When carpet is manufactured, small faults occur at random.  The number of faults in 
Premium carpets can be modelled by a Poisson distribution with mean 0.5 faults per 20 m2.
Mr Jones chooses Premium carpets to replace the carpets in his office building.  The office 
building has 10 rooms, each with the area of 80 m2.
	
(a)	
Find the probability that the carpet laid in the first room has fewer than three faults.
[3]
	
(b)	
Find the probability that exactly seven rooms will have fewer than three faults in the 
carpet.
[3]
