A team of 6 players is to be selected from 10 volleyball players, of whom 8 are boys 
and 2 are girls.
	
(a)	
In how many ways can the team be selected?
[2 marks]
	
(b)	
In how many of these selections is exactly one girl in the team?
[3 marks]
	
(c)	
If the selection of the team is made at random, find the probability that exactly 
one girl is in the team.
[2 marks]
