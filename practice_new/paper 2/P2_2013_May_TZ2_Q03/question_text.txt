It is believed that the lifespans of Manx cats are normally distributed with a mean of 
13.5 years and a variance of 9.5 years2.
	
(a)	
Calculate the range of lifespans of Manx cats whose lifespans are within one 
standard deviation of the mean.
[2 marks]
	
(b)	
Estimate the number of Manx cats in a population of 10 000 that will have a 
lifespan of less than 10 years.  Give your answer to the nearest whole number.
[3 marks]
