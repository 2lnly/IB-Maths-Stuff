time t = 0, where  t  is measured in seconds.  The acceleration,  a , measured in ms−2,
of the jet plane is given by the straight line graph below.

(a)	
Find an expression for the acceleration of the jet plane during this time, 
in terms of  t .
[1 mark]
	
(b)

velocity in ms−1 during the minute that follows.

long the jet plane is travelling greater than this speed.
