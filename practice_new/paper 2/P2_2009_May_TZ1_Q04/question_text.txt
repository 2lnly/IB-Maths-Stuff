Mr Lee is planning to go fishing this weekend.  Assuming that the number of fish 
caught per hour follows a Poisson distribution with mean 0.6, find
	
(a)	
the probability that he catches at least one fish in the first hour;
[2 marks]
	
(b)	
the probability that he catches exactly three fish if he fishes for four hours;
[2 marks]
 
(c) 
the number of complete hours that Mr Lee needs to fish so that the probability 
of catching more than two fish exceeds 80 %.
[3 marks]
