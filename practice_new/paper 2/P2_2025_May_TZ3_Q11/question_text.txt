Amanda enters data from surveys into a database. It can be assumed that the accuracy of 
any survey entered is independent of all other surveys entered. 
From previous records, it is known that Amanda enters  8 %  of the surveys inaccurately.
(a)	
On a particular day Amanda enters data from  50  surveys.
(i)	
Find the probability that Amanda entered at most six surveys inaccurately.	
(ii)	
Given that at most six surveys were entered inaccurately, find the probability that 
exactly four surveys were entered inaccurately.
[5]
On a different day Amanda enters data from  n  surveys. On this day, the probability that at 
most six surveys were entered inaccurately is approximately  0.367 .
(b)	
Find the value of  n .
