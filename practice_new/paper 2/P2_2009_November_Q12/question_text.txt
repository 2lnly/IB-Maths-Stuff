(a)	
The circular Ferris wheel has a radius of 10 metres and is revolving at a rate 
of 3 radians per minute.  Determine how fast a passenger on the wheel is going 
vertically upwards when the passenger is at point A, 6 metres higher than the 
centre of the wheel, and is rising.
[7 marks]
	
(b)	
The operator of the Ferris wheel stands directly below the centre such that the 
bottom of the Ferris wheel is level with his eyeline.  As he watches the passenger 
his line of sight makes an angle α  with the horizontal.  Find the rate of change 
of α  at point A.
[3 marks]
