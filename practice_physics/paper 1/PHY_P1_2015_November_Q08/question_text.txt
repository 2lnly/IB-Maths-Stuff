An ideal gas and a solid of the same substance are at the same temperature.  The average 
kinetic energy of the gas molecules is Eg and the average kinetic energy of the solid molecules 
is Es.  What is the comparison between Eg and Es?
	
A.	
Eg is less than Es.
	
B.	
Eg equals Es.
	
C.	
Eg is greater than Es.
	
D.	
The relationship between Eg and Es cannot be determined.
