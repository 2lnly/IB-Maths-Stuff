A fixed mass of an ideal gas expands slowly at constant temperature in a container.
Three statements about the gas molecules during the expansion are:
I.	
They collide with the walls of the container at a reduced rate.
II.	
They travel further on average between each collision.
III.	
Their average kinetic energy decreases as the gas expands.
Which statements are correct?
A.	
I and II only
B.	
I and III only
C.	
II and III only
D.	
I, II and III
