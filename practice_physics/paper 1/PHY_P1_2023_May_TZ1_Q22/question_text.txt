Three claims are made about the structure of the atom.
I.	
Most of the atom is empty space.
II.	
The positive charge of the atom is concentrated in a small volume.
III.	
The electrons have discrete energy levels.
Which of these claims can be deduced from the Rutherford-Geiger-Marsden scattering 
experiment?
A.	
I and II only
B.	
I and III only
C.	
II and III only
D.	
I, II and III
