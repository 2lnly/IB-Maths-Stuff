Three statements about fossil fuels are:
I.
There is a finite amount of fossil fuels on Earth.
II.
The transfer of energy from fossil fuels increases the concentration of CO2
in the atmosphere.
III.
The geographic distribution of fossil fuels is uneven and has led to
economic inequalities.
Which statements justify the development of alternative energy sources?
A.
I and II only
B.
I and III only
C.
II and III only
D.
I, II and III
