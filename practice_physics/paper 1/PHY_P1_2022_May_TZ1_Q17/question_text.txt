A standing wave is formed on a string. P and Q are adjacent antinodes on the wave. Three 
statements are made by a student:
I.	
The distance between P and Q is half a wavelength.
II.	
P and Q have a phase difference of π rad.
III.	
Energy is transferred between P and Q.
Which statements are correct?
A.	
I and II only
B.	
I and III only
C.	
II and III only
D.	
I, II and III
