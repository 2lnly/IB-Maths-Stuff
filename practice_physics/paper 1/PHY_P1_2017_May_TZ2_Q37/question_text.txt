The following changes are considered.
	
	
I.	
Increase the intensity of the incident light
	
	
II.	
Increase the frequency of light
	
	
III.	
Decrease the work function of the surface

A.	
I and II only
	
B.	
I and III only
	
C.	
II and III only
	
D.	
I, II and III
