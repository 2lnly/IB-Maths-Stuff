27.	
This question is about cosmology and the early universe.
	
After its formation the universe expanded rapidly, cooling as it did so.
	
(a)	
At one stage in its development, the universe contained almost equal numbers of particles 
and antiparticles.  Observation of the recent universe suggests that the antiparticles have 
largely disappeared.  Suggest a mechanism for this observation.
