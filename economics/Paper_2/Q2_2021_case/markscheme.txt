      Command term
      “Discuss” requires candidates to offer a considered and balanced review that
      includes a range of arguments, factors or hypotheses. Opinions or conclusions
      should be presented clearly and supported by appropriate evidence.

      Benefits of the depreciation may include:
       The depreciation would help close the current account deficit (paragraph ) by
        making imports more expensive and exports cheaper
       Exported services would become cheaper in foreign currency terms which may lead
        to higher demand (but will not encourage diversification of exports (paragraph ))
       The cheaper export services might help capture other markets (in addition to US
        and Europe) (paragraph )
       Other currencies have also depreciated and hence the depreciation keeps Indian
        exports competitive (paragraph ).

      Limitations/disadvantages of the depreciation may include:
       The depreciation would make imported oil even more expensive. Given that
         demand is likely to be inelastic and since rising oil prices are already a
         contributor to the current account deficit (paragraph ), that would worsen the
         deficit
       Higher prices for imported capital goods and oil would worsen cost-push
         inflation (paragraphs  and )
       Higher prices for imported capital goods may reduce imports of such goods and
         slow down potential growth (paragraph )
       The inflation rate has risen between 2017 and 2018 (Table 1) and hence the
         depreciation would only lead to further increase in prices
       With the current high growth rate (Table 1), an increase in exports could lead to
         further demand-pull inflation.

      Any reasonable discussion.
