(a)   Explain that when producer surplus and consumer surplus are maximized,
           allocative efficiency is achieved.                                     [10 marks]

           Answers may include:
            definitions of consumer surplus, producer surplus, allocative efficiency
            diagram (demand and supply) to show producer and consumer surplus and
             allocative efficiency
            an explanation that consumer surplus is the difference between the price the
             consumer is willing to pay for a good and its selling price; an explanation that
             the producer surplus for a good is the difference between the price the producer
             is willing to sell the good for and its selling price; an explanation that the sum
             of consumer and producer surplus is maximized in competitive market
             equilibrium and allocative efficiency is achieved
            example(s) of where maximized consumer and producer surplus leads to
             allocative efficiency.
