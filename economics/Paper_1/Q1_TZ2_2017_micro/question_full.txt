(a) [10 marks]
Explain why an increase in incomes over time may lead to an increase in demand for
           some goods but a decrease in demand for other goods.

(b) [15 marks]
The income elasticity of demand for primary commodities tends to be relatively low,
           while the income elasticity of demand for manufactured goods and services tends to be
           higher. Examine the likely effects of this for individual producers and for the economy
           as a whole.
